Three steps, no scheduling

Write the test

Paste the address of the site or prototype, add the tasks you want people to try, and a few questions for afterwards.

Share the link

Send it to customers, teammates or a recruited panel. They record in their own browser, whenever suits them.

Watch and learn

Review each session with a timeline, drop timestamped notes, tag problems, and read the cross-session summary.

Built for the moments that matter

Most insight lives in a few seconds of confusion. Aloud keeps those moments easy to find and easy to share with the people who need to see them.

  • Screen and microphone recording that uploads as the session goes, so nothing is lost if a tab closes
  • A floating task panel that stays with the tester while they browse
  • Task outcomes and completion times captured automatically
  • Notes pinned to the exact second, tagged as problem, insight or positive
  • Rating and open-answer follow-ups, summarised across testers
